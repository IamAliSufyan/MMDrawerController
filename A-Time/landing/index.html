Why PaceBar

A timer that lives on your talk — not beside it.

Not a second screen

No iPad on a stand, no extra monitor to glance at. The timer sits on the same screen as your content.

Not buried in a window

You never alt‑tab away from your slides to check the time. The bar is always visible, never in the way.

Not just a number

Features

Everything you need to nail your timing.

On‑pace at a glance

Green to amber to red — then overrun.

The active section eases to amber as it runs low and flashes red in the final seconds. Go past your slot and PaceBar keeps counting up in red (+1:12), so you always know exactly how far over you are.

  • Per‑section warning colors
  • Overrun count‑up you can't miss
  • Soft chime at each section, a final cue at the end
