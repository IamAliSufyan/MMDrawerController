01 — Why a bar, not a clock

Most timers make you look away.
PaceBar doesn't.

On your slides, not a second screen

No iPad on a stand, no confidence monitor to find. The timeline sits on the same screen as your content.

Always visible, never in the way

A slim strip below the menu bar — you never alt‑tab away from your slides to check the time.

Shows pace, not just a number

Sections, colours and warnings tell you whether you're ahead, on track, or running long.

02 — What's in it

Small app. Everything you need to nail the timing.

F1

Sectioned timeline

Split your talk into parts; each fills with its own colour as you move through it.

F2

Warning colours & overrun

Amber as a section runs low, red in the final seconds — and it counts up in red if you go over.

F3

Click‑through overlay

Floats above every app, even full‑screen. Only the controls are clickable; everything else passes through.

F4

Keyboard control

Space to pause, ⌘R to restart, ⌘→ / ⌘← to jump sections, Esc to stop — without touching the trackpad.

F5

Multi‑monitor

Show it on the main screen, on all screens, or on one specific display. Drag and resize to taste.

F6

3·2·1 countdown

A clean, silent full‑screen countdown rolls before every run, so you start exactly when you mean to.

F7

Sounds & notifications

A soft chime at each section, a final cue and a macOS notification when time is up.

F8

Private & offline

Timers live on your Mac. No sign‑up, no internet, nothing uploaded, ever.

03 — On pace at a glance

Green. Amber. Red. Over.

The active section eases to amber as it runs low and flashes red in the final seconds. Go past your slot and PaceBar keeps counting up in red — so you always know exactly how far over you are.

  • Per‑section warning colours
  • An overrun count‑up you can't miss
  • Soft chime at each section, a final cue at the end

05 — How it works

Three steps.

1

Build a timer

Name it, add sections with durations and colours — or enter a total and let PaceBar split it evenly.

2

Press play

A 3·2·1 countdown rolls, then the bar appears just below your menu bar, above everything.

3

Present

Sections fill in colour, warnings keep you on pace, and the controls stay one click away.
