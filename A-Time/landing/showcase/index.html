The overlay

A bar you can read at a glance.

One horizontal bar, split into your sections. Each fills with its own colour as you go, with the current section name, its time left, and the total — and a control cluster that's the only clickable part.

  • Drag to move · drag the edge to resize
  • Light & dark, automatically
  • Pause, restart, stop & settings, one click away
Build a talk

Plan your timing in under a minute.

Name your timer, add sections with a slider or exact minutes and seconds, pick colours, and drag to reorder. Enter a total and PaceBar splits it for you — or warns you if the parts don't add up.

  • Section slider + precise mm:ss
  • Drag to reorder · auto colour palette
  • Smart total / section reconciliation

Right from the menu bar

Your timers, one click away.

PaceBar lives in the menu bar — no Dock clutter. Click the colourful icon for a compact popover: start any saved timer, or jump straight into editing. The remaining time shows right beside the icon while you present.

Never run over

Green to amber to red — then overrun.

The active section eases to amber, flashes red near the end, and if you go past your slot the bar keeps counting up in red so you know exactly how far over you are.
